Data & credits

Slop Davos is built from open government data and OpenStreetMap. It is an unofficial project, not affiliated with swisstopo or the Gemeinde Davos.

  • swissBUILDINGS3D 3.0: buildings and roofs.
    swissALTI3D: the terrain.
    SWISSIMAGE: the orthophotos on the ground and the roofs.
    swissTLM3D (vector tiles): single trees and woodland.
    swissSURFACE3D Raster: the bridge decks.
    © swisstopo, Federal Office of Topography swisstopo (terms of use)
  • swissALTIRegio: the surroundings, out to the Alps.
    Bundesamt für Landestopografie swisstopo; Tarquini S., I. Isola, M. Favalli, A. Battistini, G. Dotta (2023). TINITALY, a digital elevation model of Italy with a 10 meters cell size (Version 1.1). Istituto Nazionale di Geofisica e Vulcanologia (INGV). https://doi.org/10.13127/tinitaly/1.1; DGM Österreich, geoland.at; DGM1, Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung – www.geodaten.bayern.de; DGM 1 Baden-Württemberg: LGL, www.lgl-bw.de, dl-de/by-2-0; RGEAlti, Institut National de l'information géographique et forestière, données originales téléchargées sur https://geoservices.ign.fr/rgealti#telechargement5m, mise à jour du juillet 2023
  • OpenStreetMap: streets, rail lines, building footprints, trees, parks and water; the downhill ski runs (piste:type=downhill, with their difficulty) for the winter pistes, skiers and snowboarders.
    © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL)
  • Winterwanderwege (ch.astra.winterwanderwege): the signposted winter hiking trails, their pink markers and the winter hikers.
    © Bundesamt für Strassen ASTRA / swisstopo, SchweizMobil, Kanton Graubünden; via geo.admin.ch, opendata.swiss terms of use (open use, must provide the source)

Logos: Marken der jeweiligen Inhaber; Quellen siehe assets/logos/SOURCES.md (Wikimedia Commons).

Gebäudenutzung, Geschosse und Baujahre der Fassaden: Eidg. Gebäude- und Wohnungsregister (GWR), © Bundesamt für Statistik BFS, Open Government Data.

Fassadenfarben aus Strassenbildern: Mapillary-Mitwirkende, CC BY-SA 4.0.

VBD bus model: “Low Poly Mercedes-Benz Citaro 530” by giosassets (Sketchfab), CC BY 4.0, recoloured, tyres simplified.

VW T3 model: “VW T3 Van scale1-100 lowpoly for 3D-printing” by fishermans garage (Sketchfab), CC BY 4.0, rescaled, wheels removed, recoloured.

Close-up trees grown with EZ-Tree by Dan Greenheck (MIT; licence).
